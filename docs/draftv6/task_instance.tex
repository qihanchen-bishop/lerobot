\subsubsection{Task and Platform}
% 平台采用两条SO-ARM101机械臂，右臂末端安装长板工具，前侧固定相机观察桌面。
The platform consists of two SO-ARM101 arms, a paddle tool attached to the right arm, and fixed front and side cameras observing the tabletop workspace.
% 示范包含方块、纸团、螺丝螺母三类目标，初始由同色可形变布料遮挡。
Demonstrations involve three object categories: cubes, crumpled paper, and screws/nuts, initially covered by deformable cloth of one color.
% 先暴露并分离，再推入指定区域，最后恢复前视画面中的布料覆盖率。
The arms first expose and separate the object from the cloth, push it into a designated region, and then restore cloth coverage in the front view.

% 用完整目标投影、目标区域足迹和布料投影定义完成条件。
Let $O_t^\star$, $R^\star$, and $C_t$ denote the complete object projection, full target-region footprint, and cloth projection, respectively.
The placement and restoration conditions are
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 p_{\mathrm{in}}(t)&=\ind\{O_t^\star\subseteq R^\star\},\\
 p_{\mathrm{restore}}(t)&=
 \ind\{|C_t\cap\Omega|/|\Omega|\ge\theta_C\},
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:taskpredicates}
\end{equation}
% Omega为整幅前视图像域，theta_C为布料覆盖阈值。
where $\Omega$ is the full image domain of the fixed front camera and $\theta_C$ is the required cloth-coverage ratio.
% 两项条件须在时限内同时成立。
The task is complete when both conditions hold within the time limit:
\begin{equation}
 \Phi(s_{0:T})=
 \ind\{\exists\,t\le T:
 p_{\mathrm{in}}(t)=1\land p_{\mathrm{restore}}(t)=1\}.
 \label{eq:completiontask}
\end{equation}
