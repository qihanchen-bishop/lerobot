This section studies conditions for improving task performance from limited demonstrations.
We first relate imitation error to task failure and examine the limitations of coverage over complete histories.
Representation compression may improve coverage, but conditional action-error discrepancies also affect risk transfer.
We then assess information preservation through decision loss and examine when new observations and execution history yield beneficial, timely corrections.
