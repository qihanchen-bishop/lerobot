\section{Method Details}
\label{app:methoddetails}

\subsection{Descriptor Labels and Quality Weights}
\label{app:seguncertainty}
\label{app:visibilityextension}
% 质量依据描述量所需的类别和几何量传播，避免无关类别影响有效监督。
Descriptor supervision uses the quality evidence relevant to its geometric definition rather than a single frame-wide score.
% 举例说明面积、位置及工具端点依赖不同的分割证据。
Visible area depends primarily on target extent and topology; relative position also depends on the localization of both objects, and a tool-endpoint vector is sensitive to tool shape.
% 类别准入、描述量有效性及软降权共同确定权重。
For the classes $\mathcal C_j$ used by descriptor $j$, let $\eta_{t,c}^{\mathrm{seg}}$ indicate that class $c$ passes anomaly screening, and let $m_{t,j}^{G}$ indicate that the descriptor is defined.
The supervision weight is
\begin{equation}
 \varpi_{t,j}^{G}
 =m_{t,j}^{G}
  \prod_{c\in\mathcal C_j}\eta_{t,c}^{\mathrm{seg}}\,
  g_j(\upsilon_{t,j}^{G}),
 \label{eq:relationadmission}
\end{equation}
% 异常程度从相关证据汇总，g单调降权而非正确概率。
where $\upsilon_{t,j}^{G}$ aggregates the relevant evidence in~\eqref{eq:segevidence}, and $g_j$ is a nonincreasing weight between zero and one.
% 可信范围全权重，存疑降权，异常拒绝；人工审核确定边界。
Reviewed descriptor labels determine the reliable, downweighted, and rejected ranges; these weights are not probabilities of correctness.
% 缺少连续标定时仅使用准入，不凭空解释软分数。
Without sufficient evidence to calibrate a graded weight, $g_j\equiv1$ gives validity and anomaly admission alone.
% 所有组统一降权会被组内归一化抵消，主要作用是调整样本相对权重。
The groupwise normalization in~\eqref{eq:methodbaseloss} primarily changes the relative contributions of samples within a descriptor group.

% 区分不可见与漏分，未定义关系不以零距离填充。
An empty mask is a valid zero-area label only when absence is confirmed by review or supporting temporal evidence.
Undefined distances and directions are excluded from the auxiliary loss rather than labeled as zero.
% 缺少光流对应不等价于错误，过度结构修补则不进入分割训练。
Missing optical-flow correspondences provide no motion evidence, whereas excessive structural repair excludes a frame from segmentation training.
% 离线诊断可使用完整视频，但不作为在线可用信息。
Whole-video diagnostics may use later frames offline; they are not supplied to the deployed policy.

\subsection{Pseudo-Label and Descriptor-Readout Errors}
\label{app:pseudolabel}
% 使用与C一致的分布索引，以三角不等式分离拟合与标签误差。
For either evaluation distribution $\rho$ used in Section~\ref{sec:representationanalysis}, inserting the descriptor pseudo-label $\widetilde G$ gives
\begin{equation}
 \delta_{G,\rho}
 \le \E_\rho\norm{\widehat G-\widetilde G}
    +\E_\rho\norm{\widetilde G-G}.
 \label{eq:pseudolabel}
\end{equation}
% 第一项是读出相对标签的误差，第二项是标签本身误差；质量筛选主要针对后者。
The terms separate descriptor fitting from pseudo-label error, motivating quality screening in addition to query supervision.
% 准入后的精度并不代表所有历史，需记录有效率。
When measured only on admitted labels, this comparison concerns the admitted subset; the admission rate is reported alongside the error.

\subsection{Task-Descriptor Instance}
\label{app:descriptorinstance}
% 当前实例仅从主视角计算几何描述量，不混合视角的像素坐标。
Our task instance uses front-view masks for three descriptor groups, irrespective of whether the policy receives one or two camera views.
% 三组分别为可见面积、目标区域距离和工具端点到目标的向量。
They describe target visibility, target--region distance, and the vector from a tool endpoint to the target.
% 定义图像尺寸、掩码与质心。
For image width $W$ and height $H_I$, let $M_O,M_R,M_T$ be the object, region, and tool masks, with centroids $c_O,c_R,c_T$ when nonempty.
The labels are
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \widetilde G^{(1)}&=|M_O|/(WH_I),\\
 \widetilde G^{(2)}&=\norm{c_O-c_R}/
       \sqrt{(W-1)^2+(H_I-1)^2},\\
 \widetilde G^{(3)}&=\clip(\bar c_O-\bar e_{T,L},-1,1).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:methodgeometry}
\end{equation}
% 归一化像素坐标，保留方向符号。
Here, $\bar c=(c_x/(W-1),c_y/(H_I-1))$, with rightward and downward positive directions.
% 工具端点由主轴极值及左侧约定确定，不等同于物理接触端。
The endpoint $\bar e_{T,L}$ is the leftmost of the two extremes along the tool mask's principal axis in normalized coordinates.
This geometric convention need not identify the physical contact tip.
% 区域质心距离用于辅助描述，不是任务完成判据。
Target--region centroid distance is an auxiliary descriptor, not the criterion for complete target containment.

\subsection{Evaluation Scope}
\label{app:datascope}
% 种子见过初始教师，作为参考而非独立最终测试。
Seed videos used by the initial segmenter provide a development reference, not an independent final test.
% 最终跨视频评价按完整视频隔离所有训练与选择过程。
Cross-video evaluation requires held-out videos excluded from annotation-model fitting and selection, with the split maintained through policy training.
Descriptor evaluation uses the base policy in deployment mode.
For ACT, this sets the latent input to zero rather than conditioning on the demonstrated action posterior~\cite{zhao2023act}.
